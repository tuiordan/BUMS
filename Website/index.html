Built for Every Fitness Journey

Who Is RepRight For?

RepRight is designed for anyone who wants to improve their exercise technique and get more out of their workouts. Whether you're a beginner learning proper form, an experienced lifter refining your technique, or an athlete working toward your fitness goals, RepRight provides personalized feedback to help you understand your movements.

It's also useful for people who train at home without access to a personal trainer, as well as coaches and fitness professionals who want an additional tool to evaluate their clients' exercise technique.

Our Vision

Better Form. Better Training.

Our vision is to make personalized workout feedback accessible to everyone, helping individuals understand their movements, improve their exercise technique, and train with greater confidence.

Unlike traditional fitness apps that focus mainly on tracking sets, repetitions, and weights, RepRight focuses on movement quality by analyzing uploaded workout videos and providing clear, actionable feedback.
