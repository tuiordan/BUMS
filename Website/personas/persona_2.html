Persona

Sarah is a 25-year-old marketing coordinator living in Nashville, Tennessee. She graduated with a degree in communications and has been strength training consistently for six years.

She follows a structured lifting plan, tracks her workouts on her phone, and is comfortable using fitness wearables and workout apps. Because of her busy work schedule, she usually trains early in the morning and often works out without another person available to watch her form.

Sarah understands the fundamentals of lifting, but she finds it difficult to notice small technique changes when increasing weight or becoming fatigued. She sometimes records her sets and reviews them afterward, but she is not always sure what to look for.

Behaviors and Habits

  • Has been strength training consistently for six years.
  • Follows a structured lifting program.
  • Tracks workouts using her phone.
  • Uses fitness wearables and workout apps.
  • Often trains early in the morning.
  • Sometimes records workout sets to review her technique afterward.

Goals

  • Receive detailed feedback about her lifting technique.
  • Identify subtle changes in form as weight increases.
  • Compare her technique across different workouts.
  • Identify recurring technique problems.
  • Maintain control over which workout videos are saved or shared.

Problems and Frustrations

  • Small changes in technique can be difficult to notice during heavy sets.
  • Fatigue can affect her movement without her immediately realizing it.
  • Reviewing workout videos alone does not always make it clear what she should look for.
  • She often does not have another person available to check her technique.

What Sarah Needs from RepRight

Detailed Form Feedback

Sarah wants practical feedback that helps her identify subtle technique changes in exercises such as squats and deadlifts.

Workout Comparison

She wants to compare recordings and feedback from different sessions to understand how her technique changes over time.

Customizable Feedback

Sarah wants the option to receive concise or more detailed analysis depending on what she is working on.

Privacy Controls

She wants control over which workout videos are stored and which recordings are shared.

Related Scenarios

Sarah's scenarios explore improving deadlift technique, comparing workout recordings, and customizing the level of form feedback she receives.
