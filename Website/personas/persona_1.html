Persona

Jake is a 20-year-old sophomore at a university in Kentucky studying business administration. He recently began going to the gym four days a week to improve his strength and overall fitness.

Although Jake played recreational sports in high school, he has limited experience with weightlifting and is unfamiliar with proper technique for exercises such as squats, deadlifts, and bench presses.

Jake regularly uses his smartphone and is comfortable navigating fitness apps, but he has never used software that analyzes exercise technique.

Behaviors and Habits

  • Works out approximately four days per week.
  • Usually trains alone.
  • Uses social media videos to learn new exercises.
  • Frequently uses his smartphone and fitness apps.
  • Is still learning proper strength-training technique.

Goals

  • Improve his overall strength and fitness.
  • Learn how to perform exercises correctly.
  • Receive understandable feedback on his form.
  • Improve his technique without always needing another person to watch him.

Problems and Frustrations

  • Has difficulty determining whether he is performing exercises correctly.
  • Worries that improper technique could lead to injury.
  • Social media videos show general technique but do not provide personalized feedback.
  • Does not want complicated terminology when receiving corrections.

What Jake Needs from RepRight

Exercise Analysis

Jake wants to record his exercises and receive feedback about his movement and technique.

Simple Feedback

Feedback should explain corrections clearly without complicated fitness terminology.

Easy to Use

Jake wants an application that is easy to navigate from his smartphone.

Affordable

As a university student, Jake prefers an affordable fitness application.

Related Scenarios

Jake's scenarios explore analyzing squat technique, learning a new exercise, and tracking technique improvement over time.
