Persona

Marcus is a 21-year-old junior studying exercise science and plays soccer at a university in Tennessee. His weekly schedule includes classes, team practices, strength training, and games.

He has several years of experience in supervised athletic training and is comfortable using mobile apps and video tools used by his coaches. Because his schedule changes throughout the season, he sometimes completes strength-training sessions independently.

Marcus wants to train efficiently while maintaining good technique during demanding training weeks. When a coach is not present, he wants a way to review his movement rather than relying only on how an exercise feels.

Behaviors and Habits

  • Balances classes, soccer practices, strength training, and games.
  • Has several years of supervised athletic training experience.
  • Uses mobile apps and video tools provided by coaches.
  • Sometimes completes strength workouts without a coach present.
  • Reviews exercise technique as part of his athletic development.

Goals

  • Maintain consistent exercise technique during demanding training weeks.
  • Review movements when a strength coach is not present.
  • Identify possible technique breakdowns in recorded exercises.
  • Organize workout videos by exercise and session.
  • Discuss RepRight feedback with his coach during future training sessions.

Problems and Frustrations

  • A coach is not always available during independent training sessions.
  • It can be difficult to tell whether technique changes when he is fatigued.
  • Relying only on how a movement feels does not provide enough information.
  • He needs results quickly because of his busy athletic schedule.
  • He does not want an app to replace professional coaching or make unsupported injury-prevention claims.

What Marcus Needs from RepRight

Fast Results

Marcus wants quick and clear analysis so that RepRight fits into his busy training schedule.

Technique Review

He wants to analyze recorded exercises and identify possible changes in alignment, balance, and movement.

Organized Workout History

Marcus needs a simple way to organize videos and analyses by exercise and training session.

Coach Sharing

He wants to securely share selected workout videos and feedback with his strength coach.

Related Scenarios

Marcus's scenarios explore reviewing a strength session, checking technique consistency, and sharing RepRight results with a coach.
