Software Engineering Assignment 2

User Personas

These personas represent different types of users who may use RepRight to improve, evaluate, or coach exercise technique.

Persona 1

Jake Thompson

Beginner Lifter

A university student beginning his strength-training journey who wants simple and understandable feedback on exercise technique.

Persona 2

Sarah Mitchell

Experienced Lifter

An experienced strength-training user who wants detailed feedback, comparisons, and greater control over her workout videos.

Persona 3

Marcus Davis

College Athlete

A college athlete who wants quick technique feedback when training independently and a way to share results with his coach.

Persona 4

Emily Carter

Personal Trainer

A certified trainer who wants to review client exercise videos, add coaching notes, and monitor client progress efficiently.
