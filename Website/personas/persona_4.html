Persona

Emily is a 32-year-old certified personal trainer who works at a fitness studio in Louisville, Kentucky. She earned a bachelor's degree in kinesiology and has spent eight years coaching clients with different fitness backgrounds.

Emily leads in-person training sessions and occasionally reviews workout videos that clients send between appointments. She is comfortable using scheduling software, shared documents, and mobile video tools, but reviewing many client videos individually can take a significant amount of time.

She wants clients to practice movements consistently when she cannot supervise them in person. Emily would use RepRight as a starting point for reviewing technique, while still relying on her own professional judgment before recommending changes to a client.

Behaviors and Habits

  • Coaches clients with a wide range of fitness backgrounds.
  • Leads in-person training sessions.
  • Reviews client workout videos between appointments.
  • Uses scheduling software, shared documents, and mobile video tools.
  • Provides written coaching feedback on client videos.

Goals

  • Help clients maintain proper technique when training independently.
  • Review client exercise videos more efficiently.
  • Provide personalized coaching notes linked to specific moments in a video.
  • Monitor client progress over time.
  • Use AI feedback as a starting point while keeping professional coaching judgment central.

Problems and Frustrations

  • Reviewing many client videos individually can be time-consuming.
  • Written comments can be difficult for clients to connect with the exact moment in a movement.
  • Client videos and feedback need to stay organized by person and exercise.
  • AI-generated feedback must be accurate enough to be useful.
  • Client privacy and control over workout data are especially important.

What Emily Needs from RepRight

Client Video Review

Emily wants to review client-submitted exercise videos and quickly locate important moments in each recording.

Trainer Notes

She wants to add her own coaching comments to specific moments in a client's recording.

Client Progress Tracking

Emily wants to compare older and recent recordings so she can discuss visible technique changes with clients.

Privacy and Accuracy

Client data must remain private, and Emily wants to review AI-generated observations before using them in her coaching.
