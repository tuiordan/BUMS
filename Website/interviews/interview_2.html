RepRight User Research

Interview 2

Interview Information

Interview Date

October 14, 2026

Interviewer

Example: Tudor Iordan

Participant Description

Example: Athlete

Workout Background

1. How often do you work out, and what types of exercises do you typically perform?

Example response: I work out three times a week and usually do strength training, cardio, and bodyweight exercises.

2. How would you describe your experience level with weightlifting or strength training (beginner, intermediate, or advanced)?

Example response: I would consider myself an intermediate lifter.

3. Have you ever struggled with performing an exercise correctly or knowing whether your form was good?

Example response: Yes. I sometimes have trouble knowing whether my squat depth and back position are correct.

Current Challenges

4. How do you currently check or improve your exercise form?

Example response: I watch exercise videos online and sometimes ask a friend to check my form.

5. What is your biggest frustration when it comes to maintaining proper exercise technique?

Example response: It is difficult to notice mistakes while I am concentrating on completing the exercise.

App and Feature Ideas

6. If an app could analyze videos of your exercises and provide feedback on your form, how useful would that be to you, and why?

Example response: It would be very useful because I could receive personalized feedback instead of relying only on general workout videos.

7. What features would you want in an app like RepRight?

Example response: I would like video analysis, visual feedback, a form score, and progress tracking.

8. Would you prefer real-time feedback while exercising or feedback after uploading a recorded video? Why?

Example response: I would prefer feedback after uploading because I would rather concentrate on my workout while exercising.

Concerns and Priorities

9. What concerns would you have about using an AI-powered workout form analysis app (such as accuracy, privacy, cost, or ease of use)?

Example response: My biggest concern would be whether the app's feedback is accurate enough to trust.

10. What is the one feature that would make you most likely to use RepRight regularly?

Example response: Accurate personalized feedback would be the most important feature for me.

Additional Preferences

11. Which exercises would you most want RepRight to analyze first, and why?

Example response: Squats and deadlifts because proper form is especially important for those exercises.

12. What kind of feedback would be easiest for you to understand (written tips, visual overlays, scores, or video demonstrations)?

Example response: Visual overlays would make it easiest for me to see exactly what I am doing incorrectly.

13. Would you find it helpful to track your exercise form improvements over time? What would you want to see?

Example response: Yes. I would like to compare my previous form scores and videos with newer workouts.

14. Where and how would you most likely use RepRight (at home, at the gym, before a workout, or afterward)?

Example response: I would mainly use RepRight at the gym after completing an exercise.

15. Would you consider paying for RepRight? If so, what would make a paid version worthwhile?

Example response: I would consider paying if the app provided accurate feedback, progress tracking, and enough supported exercises.

Additional Comments

Example: No additional comments.
