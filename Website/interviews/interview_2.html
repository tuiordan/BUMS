RepRight User Research

Interview 2

Interview Information

Interview Date

October 14, 2026

Interviewer

Sam Safly

Participant Description

Division 1 College Athlete and Advanced Lifter

Workout Background

1. How often do you work out, and what types of exercises do you typically perform?

I work out 6 days and week. I typically perform an even mix of weightlifting, running, and playing soccer. I perform short distance and long distance running. I lift bodyweight barbell and free weights.

2. How would you describe your experience level with weightlifting or strength training (beginner, intermediate, or advanced)?

I would say I’m an advanced weightlifter. I have been doing it for many years and have learned many exercises.

3. Have you ever struggled with performing an exercise correctly or knowing whether your form was good?

Yes, I have when I was first learning or even now when I try to perform new exercises. It can be challenging and I need to ask for help.

Current Challenges

4. How do you currently check or improve your exercise form?

I hope to have someone with more experience correcting me but if I don’t, I try and use google to see if I can get an explanation or video explaining it. Every once in a while I might film myself to watch it back.

5. What is your biggest frustration when it comes to maintaining proper exercise technique?

I normally lift alone or in a big group so it’s hard to get any constant feedback on form. Even if I watch it back and see it was bad it’s hard to make changes to it if it’s not in the moment.

App and Feature Ideas

6. If an app could analyze videos of your exercises and provide feedback on your form, how useful would that be to you, and why?

It would be useful because I wouldn’t need someone else or to look it up to get feedback on my form. I could have reliable opinions about what good form is.

7. What features would you want in an app like RepRight?

I would want it to analyze my form and tell me where to improve and even tell me how to improve during a lift. I would also like it to have teaching videos for new lifts.

8. Would you prefer real-time feedback while exercising or feedback after uploading a recorded video? Why?

I prefer real time feedback because it is much easier to fix in in the moment rather than going back and trying it again especially if I’m not doing the exercise multiple times.

Concerns and Priorities

9. What concerns would you have about using an AI-powered workout form analysis app (such as accuracy, privacy, cost, or ease of use)?

If the app is too expensive, maybe, but I don’t have any other concerns as long as it has been shown to be accurate and tested. My only worry is it not being reliable with form of complex lifts.

10. What is the one feature that would make you most likely to use RepRight regularly?

I would use it regularly if it gave realtime, accurate feedback appropriate to my type of lifting and exercises.

Additional Preferences

11. Which exercises would you most want RepRight to analyze first, and why?

I would definitly prefer barbell back squat because that exercise form is key to prevent injury. It is very easy to injure yourself or lose your technique with fatigue or heavy weight.

12. What kind of feedback would be easiest for you to understand (written tips, visual overlays, scores, or video demonstrations)?

I think written types, visual overlays, and video demonstrations are the best way to help me adjust my form during and after lifts.

13. Would you find it helpful to track your exercise form improvements over time? What would you want to see?

Yes, it would be helpful to track my exercise form improvements over time. I would like to see a visual representation of my progress, such as charts or graphs, showing how my form has improved or where I still need work.

14. Where and how would you most likely use RepRight (at home, at the gym, before a workout, or afterward)?

I would definitly use it at home and after workouts. I would use it less in the gym because of filming others but I would try to use it.

15. Would you consider paying for RepRight? If so, what would make a paid version worthwhile?

I would consider paying for it only if it had live feedback on lifts and had a strong guarantee on the accuracy of coaching and form.

Additional Comments

I love the idea of this app and think it would be very useful for people like me, as well as so many others. Many people struggle with form, especially early on in their lifting journey, and this app could help them avoid injuries and improve their performance.
