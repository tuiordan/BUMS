RepRight User Research

Interview 4

Interview Information

Interview Date

October 15, 2026

Interviewer

Ezekiel Opie

Participant Description

Advanced Weightlifter

Workout Background

1. How often do you work out, and what types of exercises do you typically perform?

I work out five days per week and mainly focus on hypertrophy and muscle growth. I currently place extra emphasis on lower-body training because I am recovering from a knee injury.

2. How would you describe your experience level with weightlifting or strength training (beginner, intermediate, or advanced)?

I would describe my experience level as advanced.

3. Have you ever struggled with performing an exercise correctly or knowing whether your form was good?

Yes, I have struggled both with performing exercises correctly and with knowing whether my form was good.

Current Challenges

4. How do you currently check or improve your exercise form?

I usually ask a trained professional for help or search online for information about proper exercise form.

5. What is your biggest frustration when it comes to maintaining proper exercise technique?

My biggest frustration is not always having supervision or constant feedback while I am performing an exercise.

App and Feature Ideas

6. If an app could analyze videos of your exercises and provide feedback on your form, how useful would that be to you, and why?

It would be very useful because it could solve the problem of not always having supervision or constant feedback available during a workout.

7. What features would you want in an app like RepRight?

I would want the app to explain what is wrong with my form and how poor form could negatively affect my weightlifting routine. I would also want it to explain why my form is incorrect, such as whether I am using too much weight or performing the movement improperly.

8. Would you prefer real-time feedback while exercising or feedback after uploading a recorded video? Why?

I would prefer real-time feedback because it would help minimize the amount of incorrect movement I perform during an exercise.

Concerns and Priorities

9. What concerns would you have about using an AI-powered workout form analysis app (such as accuracy, privacy, cost, or ease of use)?

My main concern would be accuracy. I would want the app to accurately evaluate all of the different parts of an exercise, including form, weight load, repetitions, and other relevant movement details.

10. What is the one feature that would make you most likely to use RepRight regularly?

Real-time feedback combined with a complete analysis of the movement would make me most likely to use RepRight regularly.

Additional Preferences

11. Which exercises would you most want RepRight to analyze first, and why?

I would most want RepRight to analyze squat-related exercises. From my experience, I have noticed complaints, injuries, and difficulties when performing squats with additional weight.

12. What kind of feedback would be easiest for you to understand (written tips, visual overlays, scores, or video demonstrations)?

Written tips, visual overlays, and video demonstrations would be the easiest types of feedback for me to understand.

13. Would you find it helpful to track your exercise form improvements over time? What would you want to see?

Yes, I would find that helpful because seeing where I started compared with where I am after correcting my movement would provide additional motivation.

14. Where and how would you most likely use RepRight (at home, at the gym, before a workout, or afterward)?

I would most likely use RepRight during a workout, especially when performing a movement that I do not feel completely comfortable with.

15. Would you consider paying for RepRight? If so, what would make a paid version worthwhile?

Yes, I would consider paying for RepRight through a subscription. Since the app could provide some of the feedback that normally requires a professional to be present, I think a reasonable price would be approximately $10 to $20 per month.

Additional Comments

No additional comments.
