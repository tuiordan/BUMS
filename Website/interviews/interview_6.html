RepRight User Research

Interview 6

Interview Information

Interview Date

10/13/26

Interviewer

Sam Safly

Participant Description

College Athlete

Workout Background

1. How often do you work out, and what types of exercises do you typically perform?

I work out 5 days a week. I do a mix of cardio and strength training. For the cardio I do mid to longer distance runs and short sprints. For strength training I use barbells, free weights, machines, and body weight.

2. How would you describe your experience level with weightlifting or strength training (beginner, intermediate, or advanced)?

I would say I am advanced with weightlifting because I have been lifting for many years and have been taught the correct way to do certain exercises.

3. Have you ever struggled with performing an exercise correctly or knowing whether your form was good?

Yes, I have struggled with this when I first began to lift weights or when I try a new exercise.

Current Challenges

4. How do you currently check or improve your exercise form?

I will usually watch a video on how to perform the exercise correctly and then just do it. Sometimes I’ll look in the mirror to check on my form. I don’t usually have someone checking my form.

5. What is your biggest frustration when it comes to maintaining proper exercise technique?

My biggest frustration would be that I usually lift alone so no one is there to tell me if my form is correct.

App and Feature Ideas

6. If an app could analyze videos of your exercises and provide feedback on your form, how useful would that be to you, and why?

If there was an app that could do this, it would be very useful because I don’t usually have anyone watching me, so having the app would give me feedback on what I was doing right or wrong and how to fix my mistakes.

7. What features would you want in an app like RepRight?

I would like it to tell me what needs to be fixed and how to do that. I would also want it to tell me when I got the form right so that I know what the proper form feels like.

8. Would you prefer real-time feedback while exercising or feedback after uploading a recorded video? Why?

I would want real time feedback so that if I am preforming an exercise wrong, it would tell me so then I could fix my form for the next set and reduce my chances of injury.

Concerns and Priorities

9. What concerns would you have about using an AI-powered workout form analysis app (such as accuracy, privacy, cost, or ease of use)?

I would worry about it potentially picking up the movements of other people in the gym and not focusing on my form only. Cost would potentially be a concern but if it was proven to be reliable, I would be willing to pay.

10. What is the one feature that would make you most likely to use RepRight regularly?

I would use it regularly if it showed me the correct form compared to my own so that I can see the differences between the right and wrong form.

Additional Preferences

11. Which exercises would you most want RepRight to analyze first, and why?

I would want exercises that put stress on the back/spine like a deadlift, back squat, or RDL. I would want this because if these types of exercises are performed incorrectly, especially at heavy loads, they can lead to injury.

12. What kind of feedback would be easiest for you to understand (written tips, visual overlays, scores, or video demonstrations)?

I think visual overlays and video demonstrations would be the easiest for me to understand because I would be able to see exactly what needs to be fixed with my form.

13. Would you find it helpful to track your exercise form improvements over time? What would you want to see?

Yes, I think it would be helpful to see my progress ovetime so that I can see what is improving and is not.

14. Where and how would you most likely use RepRight (at home, at the gym, before a workout, or afterward)?

I would use it at home and at the gym so that I can see what needs to improve during my workout so that I can improve it right away.

15. Would you consider paying for RepRight? If so, what would make a paid version worthwhile?

I would consider paying for it if it could give me real time feedback, show me videos and an overlay of my form compared to the correct form, and if it had built in workout plans that I could easily follow.

Additional Comments

No additional comments.
