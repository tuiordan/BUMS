User Interview

Interview 3

Interview Information

Interview Date

October 15, 2026

Interviewer

Tudor Iordan

Participant Description

Dance Athlete and Intermediate Weightlifter

Workout Background

1. How often do you work out, and what types of exercises do you typically perform?

I work out two to three times per week. I attend Pilates classes twice a week and do a full-body workout at the gym once a week.

2. How would you describe your experience level with weightlifting or strength training?

I would describe my experience level as intermediate.

3. Have you ever struggled with performing an exercise correctly or knowing whether your form was good?

Yes, I have struggled with knowing whether I am performing certain exercises with proper form.

Current Challenges

4. How do you currently check or improve your exercise form?

I usually check my exercise form at least once during each workout.

5. What is your biggest frustration when it comes to maintaining proper exercise technique?

When I start lifting heavier weights, I feel like my form gets worse. Because of this, I am not always comfortable increasing the weight.

App and Feature Ideas

6. If an app could analyze videos of your exercises and provide feedback on your form, how useful would that be to you, and why?

It would be extremely useful because it could help me avoid problems caused by poor form. I also would not have to constantly worry about checking my form during my workouts.

7. What features would you want in an app like RepRight?

I would like an audio feature that alerts me whenever my form starts to get worse.

8. Would you prefer real-time feedback while exercising or feedback after uploading a recorded video? Why?

I would prefer real-time feedback because it would allow me to correct my form immediately. I feel like I would learn from my mistakes more effectively in real time because I could apply the correction and feel the difference right away.

Concerns and Priorities

9. What concerns would you have about using an AI-powered workout form analysis app?

My main concern would probably be the privacy of my personal information and workout videos.

10. What is the one feature that would make you most likely to use RepRight regularly?

A reminder notification encouraging me to use the app would make me more likely to use RepRight regularly.

Additional Preferences

11. Which exercises would you most want RepRight to analyze first, and why?

I would most want RepRight to analyze lunges because I sometimes have difficulty feeling the exercise in the correct muscles.

12. What kind of feedback would be easiest for you to understand?

Written or audio tips accompanied by an animation would be the easiest type of feedback for me to understand.

13. Would you find it helpful to track your exercise form improvements over time? What would you want to see?

Yes, I would find it helpful to track my progress over time because seeing that I am improving would encourage and motivate me.

14. Where and how would you most likely use RepRight?

I would most likely use RepRight both at the gym and at home.

15. Would you consider paying for RepRight? If so, what would make a paid version worthwhile?

Yes, I would consider paying for RepRight, but I would not want to pay more than approximately $40 per year.
