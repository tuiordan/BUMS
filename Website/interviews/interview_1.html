RepRight User Research

Interview 1

Interview Information

Interview Date

October 14, 2026

Interviewer

Tudor Iordan

Participant Description

Athlete

Workout Background

1. How often do you work out, and what types of exercises do you typically perform?

I work out at home about 2–3 times a week. I mostly do bodyweight exercises such as push-ups, squats, and lunges. I also use a rowing machine and do about five minutes of ab exercises.

2. How would you describe your experience level with weightlifting or strength training?

I would say I am a beginner leaning toward intermediate. I have worked out a lot in the past, but I have only been consistent for a couple of months.

3. Have you ever struggled with performing an exercise correctly or knowing whether your form was good?

I have bad knees and my squat form is horrible. Using the app would definitely help me customize exercises around my body and physical limitations.

Current Challenges

4. How do you currently check or improve your exercise form?

I mostly watch videos on YouTube, but I do not feel like they help enough.

5. What is your biggest frustration when it comes to maintaining proper exercise technique?

The more sets I do, the more pain I feel in my knees. I find it frustrating because I cannot perform the exercises properly, which prevents me from having an efficient workout.

App and Feature Ideas

6. If an app could analyze videos of your exercises and provide feedback on your form, how useful would that be to you, and why?

It would be very useful because there is a big difference between watching YouTube videos and having something that feels like a personal trainer with you. It would help me improve my form and repetitions.

7. What features would you want in an app like RepRight?

A workout planner would be useful so that I could use the app both for AI-assisted form feedback and for planning my workouts around my schedule for a particular week.

8. Would you prefer real-time feedback while exercising or feedback after uploading a recorded video? Why?

I would prefer real-time feedback because my form gets worse after many repetitions. If the AI trainer could tell me immediately when I need to correct something, I could improve the efficiency of my workout session.

Concerns and Priorities

9. What concerns would you have about using an AI-powered workout form analysis app?

My main concern would be ease of use. AI systems can have bugs, and it may take time for the app to learn enough to give accurate and useful advice.

10. What is the one feature that would make you most likely to use RepRight regularly?

A set and rep counter would be very helpful. That way, I would not need to focus on counting and could concentrate fully on my form.

Additional Preferences

11. Which exercises would you most want RepRight to analyze first, and why?

I would start with basic bodyweight exercises because they are easier to understand and there is a large amount of existing information available about them. That could provide a good foundation before adding more complicated exercises.

12. What kind of feedback would be easiest for you to understand?

Visual overlays would be the clearest for me. Videos and written tips are already widely available online, but visual overlays could provide more personalized feedback.

13. Would you find it helpful to track your exercise form improvements over time? What would you want to see?

Short-term improvement would be helpful, but long-term progress would be even more useful. Seeing that progress could help keep me motivated.

14. Where and how would you most likely use RepRight?

I would most likely use it at home during my workouts.

15. Would you consider paying for RepRight? If so, what would make a paid version worthwhile?

I would consider either a yearly subscription or a one-time payment for the type of help the app provides.
