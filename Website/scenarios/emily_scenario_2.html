Scenario

Giving Personalized Client Feedback

Scenario

Emily notices that one of her clients repeatedly shifts their weight during a lunge. She opens the client's saved analysis and moves to the part of the recording where the issue appears.

She pauses the video at the relevant moment and adds a trainer comment with a short coaching cue. Emily also adds a positive note to another part of the exercise where the client's movement is more controlled.

RepRight keeps Emily's trainer comments visually separate from the AI-generated observations so the client can clearly understand which feedback came from the application and which came directly from their coach.

Emily sends the annotated review to the client, who can revisit the comments before their next workout.

Goal

Emily wants to give specific coaching feedback directly on a client's exercise video so that the client can connect each comment with the exact moment in the movement.

RepRight Interaction

Locate the Moment

Emily moves to the exact point in the exercise where she wants to provide feedback.

Add Trainer Comment

She attaches a coaching cue to the selected moment in the video.

Add Positive Feedback

Emily can also highlight moments where the client demonstrates better movement control.

Send Annotated Review

Emily sends the reviewed exercise to the client so the feedback can be revisited before future workouts.

Related User Stories

  1. As a personal trainer, I want to pause a client's exercise video at a specific moment so that I can comment on that movement.
  2. As a personal trainer, I want to attach my coaching notes to video timestamps so that clients know exactly what my feedback refers to.
  3. As a personal trainer, I want to add positive feedback as well as corrections so that clients know what they are doing well.
  4. As a personal trainer, I want my comments to be visually separate from AI observations so that clients can distinguish between the two.
  5. As a personal trainer, I want to send an annotated review to a client so that they can revisit my coaching feedback between sessions.
