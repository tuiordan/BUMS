Scenario

Comparing Workout Recordings

Scenario

Sarah suspects that her squat technique becomes less consistent near the end of a training block. She opens her RepRight history, filters her saved videos to squats, and selects recordings from an earlier week and her current workout.

RepRight places the selected videos and their feedback summaries together so that Sarah can compare the two sessions.

She checks the recordings at similar points in the movement and reviews which observations appear in both sessions.

Sarah adds a note about fatigue and training load, then saves the comparison for her next training review. The app helps her organize evidence without requiring her to remember details from every workout.

Goal

Sarah wants to compare squat recordings from different sessions so that she can evaluate changes in her technique and understand how fatigue or training load may affect her form.

RepRight Interaction

Filter Workout History

Sarah filters her saved recordings by exercise to quickly find relevant squat sessions.

Compare Recordings

She selects two recordings and reviews them together to identify differences in movement.

Compare Feedback

Sarah reviews the form observations from each session to identify issues that appear repeatedly.

Add Context

Sarah adds notes about fatigue and training load so that she can understand each recording in context.

Related User Stories

  1. As an experienced lifter, I want to select two recordings of the same exercise so that I can compare them.
  2. As an experienced lifter, I want to watch recordings side by side so that I can spot differences in movement.
  3. As an experienced lifter, I want to compare form feedback from two sessions so that I can evaluate progress.
  4. As an experienced lifter, I want to filter saved videos by exercise so that I can locate relevant comparisons.
  5. As an experienced lifter, I want to see recording dates and weights so that I can understand the conditions of each attempt.
