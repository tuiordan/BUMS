Software Engineering Assignment 2

User Scenarios

These scenarios describe realistic situations in which each RepRight persona may use the application to accomplish a specific goal.

Jake Thompson

Beginner Lifter

Scenario 1

Analyzing Squat Technique

Jake records and uploads a squat video to receive feedback about his technique.

Scenario 2

Learning a New Exercise

Jake uses RepRight's exercise library to learn Romanian deadlifts and review feedback.

Scenario 3

Tracking Technique Improvement

Jake reviews saved squat analyses to see how his technique changes over time.

Sarah Mitchell

Experienced Lifter

Scenario 1

Improving Deadlift Technique

Sarah analyzes a heavy deadlift set to identify technique changes.

Scenario 2

Comparing Workout Recordings

Sarah compares squat recordings from different training sessions.

Scenario 3

Customizing Form Feedback

Sarah changes her feedback preferences to receive more detailed technical information.

Marcus Davis

College Athlete

Scenario 1

Reviewing a Strength Session

Marcus reviews an independent strength-training session using RepRight.

Scenario 2

Checking Technique Consistency

Marcus compares recordings from different training days to evaluate consistency.

Scenario 3

Sharing Results with a Coach

Marcus securely shares a saved analysis with his strength coach.

Emily Carter

Personal Trainer

Scenario 1

Reviewing a Client Exercise Video

Emily reviews a client-submitted squat video and AI-generated observations.

Scenario 2

Giving Personalized Client Feedback

Emily adds trainer comments to specific moments in a client's exercise video.

Scenario 3

Monitoring Client Progress

Emily compares client recordings over time to support a progress review.
