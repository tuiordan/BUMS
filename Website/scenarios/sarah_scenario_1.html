Scenario

Improving Deadlift Technique

Scenario

Sarah is working through a heavy deadlift session and wants to check whether her movement changes as the load increases. She records a working set, opens RepRight, and uploads the clip under deadlift.

She chooses a detailed feedback view to examine observations about her starting position, bar path, and back position during the lift.

RepRight marks the relevant points in the recording, allowing Sarah to move between observations and the associated video frames.

She reviews the suggestions, adjusts her setup for the next set, and uploads another clip. Sarah saves both analyses along with the weight used so she can refer back to the session later.

Goal

Sarah wants to identify subtle changes in her deadlift technique as the weight increases so that she can make informed adjustments during her workout.

RepRight Interaction

Detailed Analysis

Related User Stories

  1. As an experienced lifter, I want to upload a deadlift video so that I can assess my lifting technique.
  2. As an experienced lifter, I want to view feedback on my back and hip positioning so that I can identify potential form faults.
  3. As an experienced lifter, I want to replay marked moments in my video so that I can see exactly when an issue occurs.
  4. As an experienced lifter, I want specific correction suggestions so that I can adjust my next set.
  5. As an experienced lifter, I want to record the weight lifted with each video so that I can interpret technique feedback in context.
