Scenario

Learning a New Exercise

Scenario

Jake plans to add Romanian deadlifts to his workout routine, but he does not know the movement well. Before starting, he searches for the exercise in RepRight's exercise library.

He opens the exercise overview and reviews the setup guidance, a short demonstration, and common form considerations. After going through the information, Jake records a practice set using light weight.

He uploads the recording to RepRight and receives feedback linked to specific moments in the video. Jake replays one section of the recording to better understand a suggestion and then checks the exercise instructions again before trying another set.

Once he feels more comfortable with the movement, Jake bookmarks the Romanian deadlift so that he can find the exercise quickly during future workouts.

Goal

Jake wants to learn how to perform an unfamiliar exercise and use RepRight's instructions and personalized feedback to practice the movement more confidently.
