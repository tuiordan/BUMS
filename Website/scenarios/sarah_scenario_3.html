Scenario

Customizing Form Feedback

Scenario

Sarah finds that beginner-level explanations are too basic for her experience. In RepRight's settings, she selects a more detailed feedback preference and chooses the exercises she analyzes most often.

During her next lifting session, she uploads a squat video and reviews the results in the new format.

RepRight presents concise movement observations with expandable explanations instead of requiring Sarah to read lengthy general instructions.

She chooses which details to expand and saves her preferences. Sarah can still switch back to a simpler view when sharing a result with a less experienced training partner.

Goal

Sarah wants the feedback she receives to match her experience level so that she can focus on the technical information that matters most to her.

RepRight Interaction

Adjust Feedback Level

Sarah selects a more detailed feedback mode that better matches her lifting experience.

Focus on Key Checkpoints

She chooses the movement details and exercises that are most important to her current training goals.

Control Video Playback

Sarah can slow down the recording to inspect complex movements and marked moments more carefully.

Save Preferences

Sarah saves her preferred feedback settings so that she does not have to configure them every session.

Related User Stories

  1. As an experienced lifter, I want to choose concise or detailed feedback so that the analysis matches my needs.
  2. As an experienced lifter, I want to prioritize specific movement checkpoints so that I can focus on one technical goal.
  3. As an experienced lifter, I want to control video playback speed so that I can inspect complex movements.
  4. As an experienced lifter, I want explanations of the metrics used so that I can understand the feedback.
  5. As an experienced lifter, I want my feedback preferences saved so that I do not have to reconfigure them each session.
