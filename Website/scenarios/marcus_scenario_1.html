Scenario

Reviewing a Strength Session

Scenario

Marcus completes a lower-body strength workout without his team's strength coach present. He records his final set of split squats and opens RepRight after training.

He uploads the clip, selects the exercise, and requests a form review. RepRight highlights moments where his balance or alignment appears to change during the set.

Marcus checks the marked video segments and reads the accompanying explanations. He saves the recording under the day's session and adds a short note about how fatigued he felt.

At his next supervised workout, Marcus can reopen the clip and discuss the observations with his coach.

Goal

Marcus wants to review his technique after an independent strength session so that he can identify possible movement issues and discuss them later with his coach.

RepRight Interaction

Upload Workout Video

Marcus uploads the split squat recording from his strength session.

Review Technique

RepRight highlights possible changes in balance and alignment during the set.

Add Session Notes

Marcus adds a note about fatigue so that the recording has useful training context.

Discuss with Coach

Marcus reopens the saved analysis during a supervised session and discusses the observations with his coach.

Related User Stories

  1. As a college athlete, I want to upload videos from my strength workout so that I can review my technique afterward.
  2. As a college athlete, I want to label videos by exercise so that my training sessions stay organized.
  3. As a college athlete, I want to receive feedback on movement alignment so that I can identify technique issues.
  4. As a college athlete, I want to review feedback between sets so that I can adjust my next attempt.
  5. As a college athlete, I want to store each session date so that I can track when my recordings were made.
