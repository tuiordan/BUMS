Scenario

Tracking Technique Improvement

Scenario

After several weeks of lifting, Jake wants to know whether his exercise form is becoming more consistent. He opens the progress area in RepRight and selects his saved squat sessions.

The app displays his recordings in date order and summarizes the form observations from each session, with links back to the original workout videos.

Jake plays an earlier squat video beside a more recent recording and notices differences in his movement. He reviews which form issues have improved and which problems continue to appear.

He adds a short note about the adjustment that helped him most and sets a reminder to review his technique during his next squat workout. His saved workout history gives him a way to follow his progress over time.

Goal

Jake wants to compare his previous workout analyses with newer ones so that he can understand whether his technique is improving and identify form problems that continue to occur.

RepRight Interaction

Review Workout History

Jake opens his saved squat sessions and reviews previous workout analyses.

Compare Recordings

He compares an older squat video with a recent recording to identify visible changes in his technique.

Track Form Changes

Jake reviews recurring form observations and looks for improvements across multiple workout sessions.

Save Notes

Jake records a note about what helped his technique so that he can remember the adjustment during future workouts.

Related User Stories

  1. As a beginner lifter, I want my previous analyses saved by exercise so that I can find older results.
  2. As a beginner lifter, I want to compare two squat videos so that I can see how my technique has changed.
  3. As a beginner lifter, I want to view a history of form scores so that I can recognize improvement over time.
  4. As a beginner lifter, I want to see which form issues keep recurring so that I can focus on those corrections.
  5. As a beginner lifter, I want to add notes to an analysis so that I can remember what I practiced.
