Scenario

Reviewing a Client Exercise Video

Scenario

One of Emily's clients submits a squat video between scheduled training sessions. Emily opens the client's profile in RepRight and selects the new submission.

RepRight displays the workout video together with possible form observations and timestamps for the relevant moments in the movement.

Emily watches the full set herself and then reviews the highlighted moments suggested by the application. She uses the analysis as a starting point rather than automatically accepting every observation.

After reviewing the clip, Emily saves the analysis to the client's history so that she can return to it during their next appointment and discuss the movement in more detail.

Goal

Emily wants to review a client's exercise video efficiently while still applying her own professional judgment to the AI-generated observations.

RepRight Interaction

Open Client Submission

Emily opens the client's profile and selects the newly submitted exercise video.

Review AI Observations

RepRight highlights possible form observations and links them to specific moments in the recording.

Perform Trainer Review

Emily watches the complete set herself and evaluates the suggested observations using her coaching experience.

Save to Client History

Emily saves the reviewed video and analysis so that it can be discussed during a future appointment.

Related User Stories

  1. As a personal trainer, I want to open a client's submitted exercise video so that I can review their technique.
  2. As a personal trainer, I want to see AI-generated observations linked to video timestamps so that I can quickly locate important moments.
  3. As a personal trainer, I want to watch the full exercise video myself so that I can form my own professional assessment.
  4. As a personal trainer, I want AI observations to remain separate from trainer feedback so that clients can distinguish between them.
  5. As a personal trainer, I want reviewed videos saved to a client's history so that I can revisit them during future sessions.
