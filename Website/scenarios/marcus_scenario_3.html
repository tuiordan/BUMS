Scenario

Sharing Results with a Coach

Scenario

Marcus wants his strength coach to review a RepRight analysis from a recent squat session. He opens the saved analysis and selects the sharing option.

Before sharing, Marcus reviews exactly which information will be included, such as the workout video, RepRight's form observations, and any notes he added to the session.

He creates a restricted sharing link and sends it to his coach. The coach can then review the recording and its associated analysis before their next training session.

After the discussion is complete, Marcus can return to RepRight and revoke access to the shared result. This gives him control over who can view his workout data and for how long.

Goal

Marcus wants to securely share a saved workout analysis with his coach so that they can review the same information and discuss his technique together.

RepRight Interaction

Open Saved Analysis

Marcus selects the squat analysis that he wants his coach to review.

Review Shared Information

He checks which video, feedback, and notes will be included before giving someone else access.

Share with Coach

Marcus creates a restricted link so that his coach can review the saved workout analysis.

Revoke Access

Marcus can disable the sharing link after his coach has reviewed the analysis.

Related User Stories

  1. As a college athlete, I want to share a saved exercise analysis with my coach so that we can review my technique together.
  2. As a college athlete, I want to choose which video and feedback are shared so that I control my workout information.
  3. As a college athlete, I want to generate a restricted sharing link so that only intended people can view my analysis.
  4. As a college athlete, I want my coach to see the form observations attached to my recording so that we can discuss specific moments.
  5. As a college athlete, I want to revoke shared access so that I remain in control of my workout data.
