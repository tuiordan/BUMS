Scenario

Analyzing Squat Technique

Scenario

Jake is learning barbell squats and is unsure whether he reaches an appropriate depth. At the gym, he places his phone on a tripod and records a set from the side.

He opens RepRight, uploads his clip, selects the squat exercise, and starts an analysis. RepRight displays the recording alongside feedback on visible movement patterns, including squat depth and trunk position.

Jake taps an observation to see the portion of the clip it refers to. He reads the plain-language suggestions, performs a second set, and uploads the new recording.

He reviews the two analyses to check whether the changes he tried improved the issues identified. Jake saves both sessions in his history so he can revisit them during his next workout.

Goal

Jake wants to understand whether his squat technique is correct and use RepRight's feedback to make adjustments during the same workout.

RepRight Interaction

Record and Upload

Jake records his squat from the side and uploads the video to RepRight.

Select Exercise

He identifies the uploaded recording as a squat so that RepRight can analyze the correct movement.

Review Form Feedback

RepRight highlights visible movement observations such as squat depth and trunk position.

Save and Compare

Jake saves both analyses so he can review the changes between his first and second squat attempts.

Related User Stories

  1. As a beginner lifter, I want to upload a video of my squat so that I can receive feedback on my technique.
  2. As a beginner lifter, I want to label my uploaded video as a squat so that RepRight analyzes the correct movement.
  3. As a beginner lifter, I want to see feedback about squat depth so that I know whether I am lowering far enough.
  4. As a beginner lifter, I want to see possible knee and back alignment issues so that I know what to correct.
  5. As a beginner lifter, I want to save my squat analysis so that I can review it before my next set.
