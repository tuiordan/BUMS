Scenario

Monitoring Client Progress

Scenario

Emily is preparing for a monthly progress review with one of her clients. She opens the client's RepRight history and filters the saved recordings to repeated squat sessions from the past month.

She selects an older recording and a more recent one, then reviews both videos together with the related RepRight observations and her previous trainer notes.

Emily looks for changes that have remained consistent across multiple sessions rather than judging progress from only one recording.

During the client's appointment, Emily uses the comparison to discuss progress, recognize improvements, and identify a technique goal for the next stage of training. She then saves a short progress summary and the new practice goal in the client's history.

Goal

Emily wants to review a client's technique across multiple sessions so that she can evaluate long-term progress and set appropriate training goals.

RepRight Interaction

Review Client History

Emily opens the client's saved exercise history and filters recordings by exercise.

Compare Recordings

She compares older and recent recordings to identify visible changes in technique.

Evaluate Progress

Emily looks for improvements and recurring issues across several sessions rather than a single video.

Set a New Goal

Emily saves a progress summary and a new technique goal for the client's next stage of training.

Related User Stories

  1. As a personal trainer, I want to view a client's exercise history so that I can review progress across multiple sessions.
  2. As a personal trainer, I want to filter client recordings by exercise so that I can quickly find comparable videos.
  3. As a personal trainer, I want to compare older and recent client videos so that I can evaluate changes in technique.
  4. As a personal trainer, I want to review previous trainer notes and form observations so that I can understand the client's progress in context.
  5. As a personal trainer, I want to save a progress summary and new practice goal so that the client has a clear focus for future sessions.
