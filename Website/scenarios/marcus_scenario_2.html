Scenario

Checking Technique Consistency

Scenario

During a busy week of practices and games, Marcus wants to see whether his lifting technique stays consistent.

He opens RepRight's history and filters his saved squat and lunge recordings by exercise. The app shows recent sessions and the form notes recorded for each video.

Marcus compares clips from an easier training day and a more demanding one. After reviewing the differences, he writes a note to ask his coach about a repeated issue.

He uses his saved videos to guide that conversation rather than treating the app as a medical or injury diagnosis tool.

Goal

Marcus wants to compare his technique across different training sessions so that he can identify recurring movement changes and discuss them with his coach.

RepRight Interaction

Filter Training History

Marcus filters his saved recordings by exercise to quickly locate squat and lunge sessions.

Compare Sessions

He compares recordings from easier and more demanding training days to evaluate technique consistency.

Identify Recurring Issues

Marcus reviews repeated form observations to see which technique changes continue to appear.

Add Training Notes

Marcus records notes about fatigue and workload so that he can discuss the observations with his coach.

Related User Stories

  1. As a college athlete, I want to compare form results across sessions so that I can assess consistency.
  2. As a college athlete, I want to see trends for the same exercise so that I can identify repeatable technique changes.
  3. As a college athlete, I want to flag an unusually poor set so that I can revisit the recording.
  4. As a college athlete, I want to add session notes so that I can connect technique changes with fatigue or workload.
  5. As a college athlete, I want to review recurring form warnings so that I can plan what to practice.
