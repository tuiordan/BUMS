We're RepRight, an all new fitness app that tracks your form in real time and offers tips to better your lifts.

Whether your a beginner looking to improve, an athlete trying to understand an exercise, or a coach hoping to be more available to your clients, RepRight has something to offer you. 

We are 3 student-athletes who spend our offseason away from school and our strength trainers. We know how hard it can be to understand different lifts, especially when you're a beginner just getting started with working out. It is important to maintain proper form and technique to reduce injury risk, and that's exactly what we wanted in a fitness app.

You can pick exactly what exercise you've been assigned or are attempting and receive real feedback on your form and technique. It's completely judgement free and simple to use. You'll always receive a percentage index to tell you how close you are, while the app will give you real feedback about what you could do better.

So if you're ready to take the next steps in your fitness journey and get the full benefit of your lifts, RepRight is right for you!
