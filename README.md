# Part One: The Product Idea

## 1. What is the software product?

**RepRight** is a fitness and workout form analysis application that allows users to upload videos of themselves performing exercises. The application analyzes their movements and provides feedback about their form.

The goal is to help users:
- Identify mistakes in their exercise technique.
- Improve workout efficiency.
- Reduce the risk of injury caused by poor form.

**Example:**

A user uploads a video of themselves performing a squat. RepRight analyzes:
- Squat depth
- Back position
- Knee tracking
- Overall movement

Based on this analysis, the application provides suggestions to help improve exercise technique.

## 2. What are possible names of the product that you considered?

## 3. What name did you finally choose?

**Chosen Name: RepRight**

We selected this name because it clearly connects to the purpose of the application.

- **Rep:** Refers to workout repetitions.
- **Right:** Emphasizes proper form and technique.

The name is simple, memorable, and reflects the application's focus on helping users perform exercises correctly and safely.

## 4. Who are the potential customers, end users, or buyers?

The primary users of RepRight are individuals who exercise and want feedback on their workout form.

### Potential Users

- **Beginner gym users:** Individuals learning proper exercise form.
- **Experienced lifters:** Individuals looking to improve their technique.
- **Athletes:** People who incorporate strength training into their training programs.
- **Home workout users:** Individuals exercising without access to a personal trainer.
- **Personal trainers:** Professionals who want an additional tool to evaluate clients.
- **Coaches:** Professionals reviewing athletes' movement patterns.
- **Fitness enthusiasts:** Individuals who want to track improvements over time.

RepRight is especially useful for people who want exercise guidance but do not always have access to a coach or personal trainer.

## 5. What are the potential features, functions, or other important details?

RepRight may include the following features:

### 5.1 Video Upload
- Users can upload videos of themselves performing exercises.
- Uploaded videos are used for movement and form analysis.

### 5.2 Exercise Selection
Users can select the exercise they are performing.

Supported exercises could include:
- Squats
- Deadlifts
- Bench press
- Shoulder press
- Lunges
- Bicep curls
- Other common exercises

### 5.3 Form Analysis
- Analyzes body movements during an exercise.
- Compares movements against commonly accepted exercise techniques.
- Identifies possible mistakes in exercise form.

### 5.4 Form Feedback
After analyzing a video, the application provides feedback about what the user is doing correctly and what could be improved.

Examples of feedback:
- "Keep your back more neutral."
- "Your knees are moving inward."
- "Try to squat deeper."
- "Keep the bar closer to your body."

### 5.5 Visual Feedback
- Highlights important body positions or joints in uploaded videos.
- Helps users visualize potential mistakes in their movements.
- Makes feedback easier to understand.

### 5.6 Form Score
- Provides a score based on exercise technique.
- Helps users understand how closely their movements match recommended form.

### 5.7 Exercise Instructions
- Provides basic instructions for performing exercises.
- Offers useful tips about proper form and technique.

### 5.8 Progress Tracking
- Allows users to save previously analyzed videos.
- Enables comparisons between older and newer videos.
- Helps users monitor improvements in exercise technique.

### 5.9 User Profiles
Users can create profiles containing:
- Fitness goals
- Experience level
- Exercise history

### 5.10 Workout History
- Allows users to review previously uploaded videos.
- Stores previous form analysis results and feedback.
- Helps users track their exercise history.

### 5.11 Safety Recommendations
- Identifies potentially risky movement patterns.
- Encourages users to correct their technique.
- May recommend reducing weight or consulting a qualified trainer when appropriate.

# Product Vision Statement

For individuals who want to improve their exercise technique and train more safely, **RepRight** is a fitness application that analyzes uploaded workout videos and provides clear, easy-to-understand feedback on exercise form.

Unlike traditional workout applications that primarily track sets, repetitions, or weight, **RepRight focuses on movement quality**, helping users improve their technique, make workouts more effective, and reduce the risk of injury.
