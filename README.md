Part One: The Product Idea

1. What is the software product?


Our software product is a fitness and workout form analysis application. The app allows users to upload a video of themselves performing an exercise. The application then analyzes
the movement and provides feedback about their form. The goal of the app is to help users identify mistakes in their exercise technique, improve workout efficiency, and reduce the risk
of injury caused by poor form. For example, a user could upload a video of themselves performing a squat. The app could analyze things such as squat depth, back position, knee tracking,
and overall movement, then provide suggestions for improvement.

FormFix,

RepRight.

3. What name did you finally choose?

We chose the name RepRight. We selected this name because it clearly connects to the purpose of the app. “Rep” refers to workout repetitions, and “Right” emphasizes proper form and
technique. The name is simple and reflects the app’s focus on helping users perform exercises correctly and safely.


4. Who are the potential customers, end users, or buyers?

The main users of RepRight would be people who exercise and want feedback on their workout form.

Potential users include:

Beginner gym users who are still learning proper exercise form.

Experienced lifters who want to improve technique.

Athletes who use strength training as part of their training.

People who work out at home without access to a personal trainer.

Personal trainers who want another tool to help evaluate clients.

Coaches who want to review athletes’ movement patterns.

Fitness enthusiasts who want to track improvement over time.

RepRight would be especially useful for people who want guidance but do not always have a coach or trainer available.



5. What are the potential features, functions, or other important details?



Some possible features of RepRight include:

Video upload

Users can upload a video of themselves performing an exercise

Exercise selection

Users can choose which exercise they are performing, such as:

Squat

Deadlift

Bench press

Shoulder press

Lunges

Bicep curls

Other common exercises

Form Analysis

The app analyzes body movement and compares it to commonly accepted exercise form.

Form Feedback

After analysis, the user receives feedback about what they are doing well and what needs improvement.

Examples include:

“Keep your back more neutral.”

“Your knees are moving inward.”

“Try to squat deeper.”

“Keep the bar closer to your body.”

Visual Feedback:

The app could highlight important body positions or joints in the uploaded video so users can better understand where issues occur.

Form Score:

Users could receive a score that shows how closely their movement matches recommended technique.

Exercise Instructions:

The app could provide users with basic instructions or tips on proper exercise form.

Progress Tracking:

Users could save old videos and compare them to newer ones to see improvements over time.

User Profiles:

Users could create profiles that include fitness goals, experience level, and exercise history.

Workout History:

Users could review previously uploaded videos and the feedback they received.

Safety Recommendations:

The app could identify risky movement patterns and encourage users to adjust technique, lower weight, or seek help from a qualified trainer.


Product Vision Statement


For individuals who want to improve their exercise technique and train more safely, RepRight is a fitness application that analyzes uploaded workout videos and provides clear,
easy-to-understand feedback on exercise form. Unlike traditional workout apps that mainly track sets, reps, or weight, RepRight focuses on movement quality so users can improve technique,
make workouts more effective, and reduce the risk of injury.
